# Ordering & Prep Preferences

These preferences should be applied when ordering groceries or planning meal prep.

- **Feta cheese** — do not include in recipes or shopping lists

## Prep Preferences
- No pre-cut preferences — Diego cuts his own vegetables now.

## Spice & Heat
- **Default to mild/low heat** for condiments and sauces (salsa, hot sauce, etc.). Diego has his own ways to spice things up if needed.

## Ordering Notes
- Order via DoorDash grocery delivery
- No brand preference — whatever is available and reasonably priced

## Chrome Extension Ordering Rules

### DO NOT take screenshots
Taking screenshots/images freezes execution. Never use screenshot or image capture tools during ordering. Read page content via text/DOM only.

### Avoid duplicate items
When an item appears multiple times on the list (e.g., "3 lbs chicken thighs" total), add the **same product** multiple times or adjust quantity — do NOT pick two different products. If you need 3 lbs and the store sells 1.5 lb packs, add 2x of the **same** pack.

### Validate the cart before checkout
Before finishing, read the cart contents as text and verify:
1. Every item on the shopping list has a match in the cart
2. No item appears more than intended (no accidental duplicates)
3. Quantities are correct
4. No items were added that aren't on the list

If something looks wrong, fix it before proceeding. Do not ask to take a screenshot to verify — read the cart text instead.
