# Ordering & Prep Preferences

These preferences should be applied when ordering groceries or planning meal prep.

## Disliked Ingredients
- **Feta cheese** — do not include in recipes or shopping lists
- **Papaya** — do not include in fruit bowls or recipes

## Health Notes
- **No gallbladder** — Diego has reduced fat tolerance and can get GI distress from high-fat foods, especially full-fat dairy. Recipes should call for light/reduced-fat dairy substitutes (cream cheese, sour cream, etc.) where applicable.
- **Instacart substitution risk** — Instacart has substituted full-fat cream cheese for a recipe that called for light/reduced-fat, which caused GI issues. Always double-check dairy substitutions before checkout or at delivery, especially for cream cheese, sour cream, and yogurt.
- **ADHD** — favor lower-glycemic, blood-sugar-stable carb choices where reasonable (sprouted/whole grain or sourdough bread over white/refined, pairing carbs with protein or fat). Not a strict requirement, just a preference to lean into when there's an easy swap.

## Prep Preferences
- No pre-cut preferences — Diego cuts his own vegetables now.
- **Carrots:** order long/whole carrots, not baby carrots — needed for good yield with the juicer.

## Spice & Heat
- **Default to mild/low heat** for condiments and sauces (salsa, hot sauce, etc.). Diego has his own ways to spice things up if needed.

## Ordering Notes
- Order via Instacart (connected MCP) — switched from DoorDash as of July 2026
- King Soopers is the default retailer (Denver, CO — 3750 Blake St)
- No brand preference — whatever is available and reasonably priced
