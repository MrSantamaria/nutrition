## Prep Day Overview (single session — no crockpot this week)

No creamy/shredded dish to reheat this week, so no mushy-leftover risk. Salmon is portioned raw on prep day but cooked fresh each night in the air fryer (~8 min, unattended) — this is the fix for texture: salmon dries out and turns rubbery if batch-cooked and reheated over several days.

**New this week:** a whole-wheat lemon parmesan pasta side (`containers/bases/lemon-parmesan-pasta.md`) rotates in for cucumber salad on 3 dinner nights — a genuine comfort-food addition to the system per Diego's request for "some enjoyment during the day," not just health-optimized bowls. A cauliflower-blend mashed potatoes recipe (`containers/bases/garlic-mashed-potatoes.md`) was also added to the system to alternate with pasta in future weeks. To stay at the 10-container cap, **hummus is dropped this week** to make room for the pasta container.

## Prep Day — Monday August 3 (~60 min active)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Start rice cooker.** 2 cups rice, plain water — stir in olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt once done. |
| 0:05 | **Roasted veggies.** Chop bell peppers (3 this week, no zucchini), red onion. Toss with oil, oregano, salt, pepper. Roast 425°F for 25–30 min, flip halfway. |
| 0:10 | **Couscous.** Boil water, pour over 2 cups dry couscous, cover 5 min, fluff with oil/parsley/salt. |
| 0:20 | **Cucumber salad.** Chop cucumbers, halve cherry tomatoes, dice red onion. Toss with oil, vinegar, salt, pepper. |
| 0:30 | **Pasta.** Cook 8 oz whole wheat pasta, toss with olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, parmesan (skipping the zucchini this week). |
| 0:45 | **Salmon portioning.** Rinse and pat dry all 7 fillets. Wrap individually (parchment or small zip bags). Put 3 in the fridge for the first half of the week, bag and freeze the other 4. |
| 0:55 | Fluff and portion rice (2 containers), couscous (2 containers), and pasta (1 container). |
| 0:58 | Pull veggies from oven, cool, portion into 2 containers. |
| 1:00 | Clean up. |

## Each Night — Cook Salmon Fresh (~10 min)

1. If frozen, thaw the next portion in the fridge overnight ahead of time.
2. Preheat air fryer to 400°F.
3. **Lemon garlic nights:** drizzle fillet with olive oil and lemon juice, sprinkle garlic, salt, pepper. Air fry 7–9 min until it flakes easily.
4. **Honey mustard nights:** whisk the glaze, brush half on before air frying, air fry 5 min, brush on the rest, air fry another 3–4 min. Watch closely at the end — the honey can burn fast.
