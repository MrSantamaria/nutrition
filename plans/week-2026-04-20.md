## Prep Day Schedule (~90 min)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Start crockpot.** Roughly dice onion. Dump chicken thighs, chickpeas, crushed tomatoes, onion, garlic, olives, olive oil, and spices into slow cooker. Set to LOW. |
| 0:10 | **Start rice cooker.** 2 cups rice with lemon/oil/oregano method. |
| 0:15 | **Burrito prep.** Dice bell pepper and onion for burritos. Saute veggies, warm beans. |
| 0:25 | Scramble 14 eggs in the same skillet. |
| 0:35 | **Assembly line.** Lay out 7 tortillas, fill, roll, wrap in foil. Into freezer. |
| 0:50 | Fluff rice, portion into 2 containers. Crumble sour cream into a small container. |
| 0:55 | **Clean up.** Crockpot runs on its own — stew will be ready by dinner. |
| ~6–8 hrs later | Shred chicken in crockpot, stir in spinach and lemon juice. Done. |

**Active time: ~55 minutes.** The crockpot does the rest.
