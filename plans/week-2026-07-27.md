## Prep Day 1 — Monday July 27 (~70 min active, plus crockpot time)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Start crockpot stew.** Chicken thighs, chickpeas, crushed tomatoes, onion, garlic, olives, oil, spices into the crockpot. Low 6–8 hrs. |
| 0:10 | **Start rice cooker.** 2 cups rice, plain water — stir in olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt once done. |
| 0:15 | **Couscous.** Boil water, pour over 3 cups dry couscous, cover 5 min, fluff with oil/parsley/salt. |
| 0:25 | **Chickpeas.** Toss 3 cans chickpeas with oil and spices on a sheet pan, roast 400°F for 25–30 min. |
| 0:30 | **Cucumber salad.** Chop cucumbers, halve cherry tomatoes, dice red onion. Toss with oil, vinegar, salt, pepper. |
| 0:45 | Fluff and portion rice (2 containers) and couscous (2 containers). |
| 0:55 | Pull chickpeas from oven, cool, portion into 1 container. |
| 1:00 | Clean up. Stew keeps cooking — shred chicken and stir in spinach + lemon juice once it's done later today. |
