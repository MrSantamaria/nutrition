## Prep Day Schedule (~80 min)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Start rice cooker.** 2 cups white rice, plain (no lemon/herbs — neutral for curry). |
| 0:05 | **Start curry.** Dice onions, chop carrots and potatoes. Sauté onion in large pot ~5 min. |
| 0:15 | Add carrots, potatoes, and water to pot. Bring to boil, reduce to simmer, cover. |
| 0:20 | **Burrito prep.** Dice bell peppers and onion. Sauté in skillet with olive oil ~5 min. Warm black beans. |
| 0:30 | Scramble 14 eggs in same skillet. |
| 0:40 | **Assembly line.** Lay out 7 tortillas, fill, roll, wrap in foil. Into freezer. |
| 0:50 | Check curry — potatoes should be fork-tender. Turn off heat, add curry blocks, stir until dissolved. Simmer 5 min on low. |
| 0:60 | Fluff rice, portion into 2 containers. Portion curry into 2 tubs. |
| 0:70 | Clean up. Done. |

**Active time: ~70 minutes.**
