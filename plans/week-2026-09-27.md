## Prep Day Schedule (~75 min)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Rice cooker.** 3 cups rice, normal water ratio. Preheat oven to 425°F. |
| 0:05 | **Sheet pans.** Chicken thighs tossed with oil + spices on pan 1; broccoli with oil, garlic, salt, pepper on pan 2. Both in at 425°F — broccoli 18–22 min, chicken 20–25 min (to 165°F). |
| 0:10 | **Turkey bacon.** Cook in skillet in batches, ~3–4 min per side. Paper towel, then chop. |
| 0:20 | **Sausage.** Same skillet — brown and crumble, ~6–8 min. |
| 0:28 | Add diced pepper + onion, ~5 min. Stir in black beans, ~2 min. Transfer to bowl. Pull broccoli/chicken from oven when done. |
| 0:35 | **Eggs.** Whisk 14 eggs with seasoning, soft scramble on medium-low. |
| 0:45 | **Assembly line.** 7 tortillas: eggs → sausage mix → bacon → cheese. Roll tight, wrap in foil. 2 to fridge, 5 to freezer. |
| 0:60 | **Finish rice.** Fluff with olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt. Portion rice, chicken, broccoli into 2 containers each. |
| 0:70 | Clean up. Done. |
