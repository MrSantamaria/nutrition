# Week of September 12–18, 2026

**Target:** ~1,925 kcal/day | 2 meals/day
**Theme:** Girlfriend's coming over — PB powder peanut noodle bowls, beef shank bouillon soup for dinner, tortilla española for weekend brunch
**Eating out:** adjust below as needed
**Prep meals needed:** 7 bowls (meal 1) + 7 soup servings (meal 2) + 1 tortilla española (Sunday brunch, made fresh, replaces that day's bowl)

This week runs three dishes: a simple daily bowl rotation (peanut noodles +
chicken + roasted broccoli), [Beef Shank Bouillon Soup](../indulgent/beef-shank-bouillon-soup.md)
as the week's dinner, and [Tortilla Española](../indulgent/tortilla-espanola-chorizo.md)
made fresh Sunday morning as a one-off brunch. The soup and tortilla are
off-plan/indulgent (see `indulgent/`) — the peanut noodle bowl is what keeps
this week simple on the batch-prep side, and is a genuinely light option:
the sauce uses PB powder instead of jarred peanut butter, so it comes in far
lower in fat than a typical peanut noodle dish.

On the night she's actually over, make [Classic Rice Pilaf](../indulgent/classic-rice-pilaf.md)
fresh as a side for the soup — a special-occasion touch, not batched, so it
doesn't add to the container count below.

## Prep Day Schedule — Saturday (~45 min active)

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Start the soup.** Trim visible fat off the beef shank. Into the slow cooker: shank, water, 4 Goya bouillon cubes, onion, garlic, bay leaves. Set to LOW. |
| 0:10 | **Chicken thighs.** Toss thighs with oil and spices on a sheet pan. Oven 400°F, 25–30 min. |
| 0:15 | **Broccoli.** Toss with oil, garlic, salt, pepper on a second sheet pan. Oven 425°F, 18–22 min (stagger with the chicken if your oven can't fit both). |
| 0:20 | **Noodles + sauce.** Boil and cook noodles, drain and rinse briefly under cool water. Whisk PB powder with warm water, then soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, ginger, garlic, sesame oil, lime juice. Toss noodles with sauce. |
| 0:40 | Portion noodles into 2 containers. Pull chicken and broccoli from the oven once done, cool, portion. |
| ~6–7 hrs later | **Skim the soup.** Uncover and skim fat off the surface. Add carrots and potatoes, continue on LOW ~1.5–2 hrs. |
| Last 20–30 min | Add mushrooms to the soup. Once vegetables are tender, remove bay leaves, season to taste. |
| Evening | Serve the first soup portions fresh for dinner. |

## Sunday Morning — Tortilla Española Brunch (~40 min active)

Made fresh, not batched — best warm/room temp anyway. Slow-cook the sliced potatoes and onion in olive oil (~20–25 min), sauté the Spanish chorizo, fold everything into whisked eggs, cook and flip, rest, slice. See the [full recipe](../indulgent/tortilla-espanola-chorizo.md) for the flip technique.
