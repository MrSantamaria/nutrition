# Classic Rice Pilaf

**Category:** Indulgent / off-plan meal — one-off side, not batch-prepped
**Method:** Stovetop
**Yield:** ~4 servings (side portion)
**Effort:** ~25 minutes, mostly hands-off simmering

A special-occasion side for the beef shank bouillon soup — made fresh the
night she's over, not part of the week's batch prep.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 1 3/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth (or water + 1 Goya bouillon cube, to match the soup)
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp toasted slivered almonds or chopped parsley, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add onion, cook until softened, ~4 min.
2. Add rice, stir to coat in the butter, and toast for 2–3 min until the edges look slightly translucent and it smells nutty.
3. Add broth and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low, cover, and simmer 15–18 min until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
4. Remove from heat and let rest, covered, 5 min. Fluff with a fork, remove the bay leaf.
5. Garnish with toasted almonds or parsley if using. Serve alongside the soup.

## Serving

Ladle the soup over or serve the pilaf on the side — either works well since the soup has plenty of its own broth.

## Storage

Best made fresh for the occasion. If there's extra, it keeps 3–4 days refrigerated.

## Tips

- Toasting the rice in butter before adding liquid is what gives pilaf its flavor over plain steamed rice — don't skip that step.
- Keep the lid on during the simmer; lifting it lets steam escape and the rice can cook unevenly.
