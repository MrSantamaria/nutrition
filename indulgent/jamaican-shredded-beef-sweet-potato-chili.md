# Jamaican Shredded Beef & Sweet Potato Chili

**Category:** Indulgent / off-plan meal — tasty, not health-optimized
**Method:** Electric pressure cooker / multi-cooker (sauté + pressure cook)
**Yield:** ~8 servings (from 3 lb chuck)
**Effort:** Moderate — searing in batches is the only real work, the pressure cooker does the rest

Chuck roast seared hard, pressure cooked with Jamaican-style warm spices
(allspice, thyme, cinnamon, ginger, scotch bonnet), then shredded and
finished with sweet potatoes, garbanzo beans, coconut milk, and lime. Serve
over rice with cornbread on the side.

---

## Ingredients

- 3 lb beef chuck roast, cut into 1.5" cubes, only large hard fat seams removed
- Salt and pepper (light) + 1 tsp allspice, for the dry rest
- Neutral oil or avocado oil (or render the trimmed fat)
- ~1.5 shallots + 1.5 red bell peppers, chopped (or 1 yellow onion in place of the shallots)
- 9 garlic cloves, minced
- 1.5 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 habanero or scotch bonnet, seeded and minced (see Heat below)
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1.5 tsp allspice
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1.5 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1.5 tsp ground thyme
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tsp celery salt
- 1.5 tsp brown sugar (honey works)
- 2 cans (28 oz each) crushed tomatoes
- ~2 lb sweet potatoes, peeled, cut ~1"
- 2 cans (15 oz each) garbanzo beans, drained and rinsed (kidney beans also work)
- 3/4 cup light coconut milk
- Juice of 1–1.5 limes

**Toppings:** sour cream, green onions, lime wedges. Serve over rice with cornbread (below).

## Instructions

1. Season the beef with salt, pepper, and allspice. Rest 30 min, then pat dry.
2. Set the cooker to **Sauté (high)**. Sear the beef in 3–4 batches until dark brown, 5–7 min per batch — don't crowd the pot. Set aside.
3. Sauté the shallots and peppers 4–5 min, scraping up the browned bits.
4. Add garlic, ginger, habanero, all the dry spices, and the tomato paste. Stir 1 min.
5. Deglaze with the broth and scrape the bottom clean (this prevents the burn warning).
6. Return the beef. Add celery salt and brown sugar. Pour the tomatoes on top — **do NOT stir**.
7. Pressure cook on **high 50 min**, natural release.
8. Shred the beef in the pot with two forks. Skim the fat.
9. Add the sweet potatoes and beans. Pressure cook on **high 4 min**, quick release.
10. Stir in the coconut milk and lime juice. Taste and adjust salt. If it's thin, run Sauté with the lid off for 10–15 min, or stir in a cornstarch slurry.

## Lessons Learned

- 3 cups of broth made it soupy — **1 cup is right**.
- Adding chili powder and shredding the beef is what makes this chili instead of stew.
- Tomato paste and tomatoes must go in **before** the pressure cook.

## Heat

- One seeded pepper, minced = **medium**.
- Whole pepper with a slit, removed after cooking = **mild** (the default per `preferences.md` — spice it up per bowl if wanted).

## Storage

Cool in shallow containers. Refrigerate 3–4 days, or freeze — it freezes well. Flavor improves on day 2. Add sour cream per bowl, not to the pot.

**Not dog safe** — contains onion/shallot and garlic.

## Side: Honey Butter Cornbread (bake fresh, day of)

**Yield:** 8x8 pan, 9 squares

- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/2 cup butter, melted

1. Heat the oven to **400°F**. Grease an 8x8 pan or cast iron skillet.
2. Mix the dry ingredients and the wet ingredients separately, then combine until just mixed (lumps are fine).
3. Bake 20–25 min, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
