# Carrot Orange Juice

**Category:** Drink (juicer)
**Method:** Juicer
**Yield:** ~2 servings (16 oz)
**Effort:** ~10 minutes

Best made fresh right before drinking — juice separates and loses vitamin C
fast, so this isn't a batch-prep item like the rest of the system.

---

## Ingredients

- 4–5 long carrots, washed and trimmed (long carrots juice more efficiently and give a higher yield than baby carrots)
- 2 large oranges, peeled
- Optional: small piece of fresh ginger, peeled

## Instructions

1. Feed the carrots and orange segments through the juicer, alternating between the two for a more even extraction.
2. Add ginger at the end if using.
3. Stir the juice (the pulp settles) and serve immediately over ice.

## Storage

Best fresh. If needed, keeps in a sealed container in the fridge up to 24 hrs — expect some separation and vitamin loss.

## Nutrition (per 8 oz serving — estimated)

| Nutrient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | ~110 |
| Protein | ~2g |
| Carbs | ~26g |
| Fat | ~0g |

## Tips

- Buy long/whole carrots rather than baby carrots for juicing — better yield and flavor.
- Adjust the carrot:orange ratio to taste — more orange for sweeter, more carrot for earthier.
