# Pasta

**Category:** Base
**Yield:** ~8 cups cooked (2 containers)
**Calories:** ~220 kcal per cup

## Ingredients
- 1 lb dry pasta (penne or rotini hold up best to reheating)
- Salt, for the pasta water
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
2. Cook pasta to al dente per package directions (slightly under is fine — it'll soften more when reheated).
3. Drain, toss with olive oil to keep it from clumping.
4. Let cool, divide into 2 containers.

## Per Cup (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 220 |
| Protein | 8g |
| Carbs | 43g |
| Fat | 1g |

## Tips
- Slightly undercooking helps it hold up better through a week of reheats.
- Toss with a little extra olive oil or a splash of pasta water when reheating if it's dried out.
