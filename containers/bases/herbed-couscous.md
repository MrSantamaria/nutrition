# Herbed Couscous

**Category:** Base
**Yield:** ~5 cups (2 containers)
**Calories:** ~200 kcal per cup

## Ingredients
- 2 cups couscous
- 2 cups boiling water or broth
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Boil water in a pot (or use rice cooker on steam setting)
2. Pour boiling water over couscous in a bowl, cover with plate, wait 5 min
3. Fluff with fork, stir in olive oil, parsley, salt
4. Cool and divide into 2 containers
