# Garlic Mashed Potatoes (Cauliflower Blend)

**Category:** Base (comfort side)
**Yield:** ~4 cups (1 container)
**Calories:** ~180 kcal per cup

The other comfort-food rotation option alongside [Lemon Parmesan Pasta](lemon-parmesan-pasta.md) — alternate between the two week to week. Blending in cauliflower cuts the carb/calorie density and adds fiber/volume without losing the mashed-potato feel. Uses light sour cream instead of butter/cream, in line with Diego's reduced fat tolerance (see `preferences.md`) — check the label says "light" or "reduced fat" before checkout.

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lbs Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 1.5 lbs cauliflower florets (fresh or frozen)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 cup light sour cream
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1 tbsp chives, chopped (optional)
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Boil potatoes and cauliflower together in salted water until fork-tender, ~15 min.
2. Drain well — cauliflower holds extra water, so let it steam-dry a couple minutes in the colander.
3. Mash with olive oil, light sour cream, and garlic until mostly smooth (a few lumps are fine).
4. Stir in chives, salt, and pepper to taste. Let cool, store in 1 container.

## Per Cup (approximate, potato + cauliflower blend)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 180 |
| Protein | 4g |
| Carbs | 24g |
| Fat | 7g |

## Tips
- The potato:cauliflower ratio can shift toward more cauliflower for fewer carbs, or more potato for a more classic texture — 50/50 is a good starting point
- Reheats fine with a splash of milk or water to loosen it back up
