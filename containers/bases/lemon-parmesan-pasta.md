# Lemon Parmesan Pasta (Whole Wheat)

**Category:** Base (comfort side)
**Yield:** ~4 cups (1 container)
**Calories:** ~230 kcal per cup

A comfort-food base — whole wheat pasta keeps it closer to the rest of the system's macros (more fiber/protein than white pasta), and stirring in zucchini adds volume/fullness without piling on calories. Meant as an occasional stand-in for the cucumber salad side at dinner, not an every-night addition — the point is enjoyment, not just macros.

## Ingredients
- 8 oz whole wheat (or chickpea) pasta, any short shape
- 2 cups zucchini, diced small (optional but recommended for volume)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta according to package directions, drain, reserve 1/4 cup pasta water.
2. While pasta cooks, sauté zucchini in a splash of olive oil over medium heat 4–5 min until just tender (or skip and stir in raw-diced for extra crunch/volume — it softens fine in the hot pasta).
3. Toss hot pasta with olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, parmesan, and zucchini. Add a splash of reserved pasta water if it looks dry.
4. Salt and pepper to taste. Let cool, store in 1 container.

## Per Cup (approximate, pasta + zucchini + oil + parmesan)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 230 |
| Protein | 9g |
| Carbs | 34g |
| Fat | 8g |

## Tips
- Don't overcook the pasta — it'll sit in the fridge for a few days and gets mushy just like anything else if it's already soft going in
- Reheats fine with a splash of water or extra olive oil to loosen it back up
