# Lemon Herb Rice

**Category:** Base
**Yield:** ~6 cups (2 containers)
**Calories:** ~210 kcal per cup

## Ingredients
- 2 cups long-grain white rice (or basmati)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Cook rice in rice cooker with normal water ratio
2. When done, fluff and stir in olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, and salt
3. Let cool, divide into 2 containers
