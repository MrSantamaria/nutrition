# Roasted Broccoli (or Broccolini)

**Category:** Veggie
**Yield:** ~6 cups (2 containers)
**Calories:** ~90 kcal per cup

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs broccoli or broccolini, cut into florets (broccolini can be left in long spears)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1/2 tsp garlic powder)
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Toss broccoli with olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.
3. Spread in a single layer (use 2 pans if needed so it roasts rather than steams).
4. Roast 18–22 min, flipping halfway, until edges are browned and stems are fork-tender.
5. Cool and divide into 2 containers.

## Per Cup (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 90 |
| Protein | 3g |
| Carbs | 8g |
| Fat | 5g |

## Tips
- Don't crowd the pan — spread out for good browning instead of steaming.
- Broccolini cooks a bit faster than broccoli crowns — start checking at 15 min.
