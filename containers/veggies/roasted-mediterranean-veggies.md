# Roasted Mediterranean Veggies

**Category:** Veggie
**Yield:** ~6 cups (2 containers)
**Calories:** ~90 kcal per cup

## Ingredients
- 2 zucchini, chopped into half-moons
- 2 bell peppers (any color), chopped
- 1 red onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Toss all veggies with olive oil, oregano, salt, pepper on a sheet pan
3. Spread in a single layer (use 2 pans if needed)
4. Roast 25-30 min, flip halfway
5. Cool and divide into 2 containers

## Per Cup (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 90 |
| Protein | 2g |
| Carbs | 9g |
| Fat | 5g |

## Tips
- Cut veggies to similar sizes so they cook evenly
- Don't crowd the pan — spread them out for better roasting
