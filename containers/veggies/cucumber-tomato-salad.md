# Cucumber Tomato Salad

**Category:** Veggie (no-cook)
**Yield:** ~4 cups (1 container)
**Calories:** ~70 kcal per cup

## Ingredients
- 2 large cucumbers, diced
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 red onion, finely diced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Chop everything, toss together in a bowl
2. Add olive oil, vinegar, salt, pepper
3. Store in 1 container

## Per Cup (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 70 |
| Protein | 1g |
| Carbs | 6g |
| Fat | 5g |

## Tips
- Keeps well for 4-5 days in the fridge
- The freshness balances out the heavier warm components in your bowl
