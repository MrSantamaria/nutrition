# Honey Mustard Salmon

**Category:** Protein
**Yield:** 1 fillet per meal (cooked fresh, not batch-prepped)
**Calories:** ~310 kcal per fillet

Sweet-tangy glaze in the spirit of the Subway honey mustard sauce, without a mayo base. Alternates through the week with [Lemon Garlic Salmon](lemon-garlic-salmon.md) — cooked fresh per meal the same way, just a different glaze applied at cook time. No prep-day difference: the same raw portioned fillets work for either flavor, so pick the glaze the night of.

## Glaze (per fillet, scale to how many you're cooking)
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp yellow mustard
- Splash of apple cider vinegar (or lemon juice)
- Pinch of garlic powder
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions — Air Fryer

1. Preheat air fryer to 400°F.
2. Whisk glaze ingredients together in a small bowl.
3. Brush half the glaze over the fillet before air frying.
4. Air fry 5 min, then brush on the remaining glaze (adding it all up front can burn the honey).
5. Air fry another 3–4 min until it flakes easily.

## Per Fillet (approximate, salmon + glaze)

| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | ~310 |
| Protein | ~34g |
| Carbs | ~8g |
| Fat | ~15g |

## Tips
- Watch closely in the last 2–3 min — the honey glaze can go from caramelized to burnt fast in an air fryer
- If alternating nightly with lemon garlic, no need to decide which fillet gets which flavor on prep day — the portioned raw fillets are identical, just choose the glaze that night
