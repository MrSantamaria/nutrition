# Spiced Chickpeas

**Category:** Protein
**Yield:** ~3 cups (1 container)
**Calories:** ~210 kcal per cup

## Ingredients
- 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Toss chickpeas with olive oil and spices on a sheet pan
3. Roast 25-30 min, shaking pan halfway through
4. Let cool, store in 1 container

## Per Cup (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 210 |
| Protein | 11g |
| Carbs | 32g |
| Fat | 5g |

## Tips
- These also work as a crunchy topping if roasted a bit longer
- Can do this alongside the chicken on a separate sheet pan
