# Turkey Meatballs

**Category:** Protein
**Yield:** ~2 lbs cooked, ~24–28 meatballs (2 containers)
**Calories:** ~280 kcal per serving (~5 meatballs)

Sauce isn't included here — add your own marinara (or whatever you're using) when serving or reheating.

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs ground turkey (93/7)
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1/2 tsp garlic powder)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- Salt and pepper
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (optional)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients. Mix with your hands just until combined — don't overwork it or the meatballs get dense.
3. Roll into ~1.5" balls (about 24–28 from this batch). Space evenly on the sheet pan.
4. Bake 18–20 min, until internal temp hits 165°F.
5. Let cool, divide into 2 containers.

## Per Serving, ~5 meatballs (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 280 |
| Protein | 30g |
| Carbs | 8g |
| Fat | 14g |

## Tips
- Ground turkey is lean and can dry out — don't skip the egg/breadcrumb binder, it keeps them moist.
- Reheat gently in sauce (stovetop, covered, low heat) rather than microwaving dry — keeps them from toughening up.
