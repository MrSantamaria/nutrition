# Baked Chicken Thighs

**Category:** Protein
**Yield:** ~2 lbs cooked (2 containers)
**Calories:** ~230 kcal per 6 oz serving

## Ingredients
- 3 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Toss thighs with olive oil and all seasonings on a sheet pan
3. Bake 25-30 min until internal temp hits 165°F
4. Let cool, chop or shred, divide into 2 containers

## Per 6 oz Serving (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 230 |
| Protein | 36g |
| Carbs | 1g |
| Fat | 9g |

## Tips
- Thighs are forgiving — hard to overcook unlike chicken breast
- No need to marinate, the spice rub is enough
