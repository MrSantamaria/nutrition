# Lemon Garlic Salmon

**Category:** Protein
**Yield:** 7 individual fillets (cooked fresh per meal, not batch-prepped)
**Calories:** ~280 kcal per fillet

Unlike the other proteins in this system, salmon is **not batch-cooked ahead** — it reheats poorly and goes rubbery/dry over a few days in the fridge. Instead, portion raw fillets on prep day and air fry one fresh each night. This is also the fix for the "mushy leftovers" problem from crockpot dishes: nothing sits reheated for a week.

## Ingredients (per fillet, scale to 7)
- 1 salmon fillet (~6 oz) — buy quality/higher-grade, not the cheapest option
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1/2 clove garlic, minced (or 1/4 tsp garlic powder)
- Squeeze of lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

## Prep Day — Portioning Only

1. Rinse and pat dry all 7 fillets.
2. Individually wrap or bag each fillet (parchment or a small container/zip bag).
3. Store 2–3 days' worth in the fridge; freeze the rest in individual portions (thaw overnight in the fridge before cooking).

## Cook Fresh — Air Fryer (per fillet, each night)

1. Preheat air fryer to 400°F.
2. Drizzle fillet with olive oil and lemon juice, sprinkle with garlic, salt, and pepper.
3. Air fry 7–9 min (check at 7 — thickness varies) until it flakes easily.

## Tips
- Salmon is very forgiving — slightly overcooked is still good, but air frying fresh keeps it flaky instead of dried out from reheating
- Can swap for any white fish, just reduce air fry time by 2–3 min
- No need to oil the air fryer basket if it's non-stick; a light spray helps prevent sticking otherwise
