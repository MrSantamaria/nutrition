# Lemon Garlic Salmon

**Category:** Protein
**Yield:** ~4 fillets (2 containers)
**Calories:** ~280 kcal per fillet

## Ingredients
- 4 salmon fillets (~6 oz each)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. Place salmon on a lined sheet pan, drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice
3. Sprinkle garlic, salt, and pepper on top
4. Bake 12-15 min until flaky
5. Let cool, divide into 2 containers (2 fillets each)

## Per Fillet (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 280 |
| Protein | 34g |
| Carbs | 1g |
| Fat | 15g |

## Tips
- Salmon is very forgiving — slightly overcooked is still good
- Can swap for any white fish, just reduce cook time by 2-3 min
