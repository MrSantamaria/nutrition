# Hummus

**Category:** Flavor Booster (store-bought)
**Yield:** 1 tub (~2 cups, 1 container)
**Calories:** ~70 kcal per 2 tbsp

## Ingredients
- 1 tub store-bought hummus (any flavor — garlic, roasted red pepper, classic)

## Instructions
1. Buy it. Put it in the fridge. Done.

## Per 2 tbsp (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 70 |
| Protein | 2g |
| Carbs | 4g |
| Fat | 5g |

## Tips
- Store-bought is perfectly fine and saves time
- A generous scoop (~3 tbsp) adds creaminess and extra protein to any bowl
