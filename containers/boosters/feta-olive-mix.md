# Feta & Olive Mix

**Category:** Flavor Booster (no-cook)
**Yield:** ~2 cups (1 container)
**Calories:** ~150 kcal per 1/4 cup

## Ingredients
- 1 block (8 oz) feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 cup kalamata olives, halved
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano

## Instructions
1. Crumble feta into a container
2. Add halved olives, drizzle olive oil, sprinkle oregano
3. Toss gently, store

## Per 1/4 Cup (approximate)
| Macro | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | 150 |
| Protein | 5g |
| Carbs | 3g |
| Fat | 13g |

## Tips
- A little goes a long way — this is your salt/umami/fat booster
- Lasts all week in the fridge
