# Crockpot Mediterranean "Crack" Chicken

**Method:** Slow cooker (low 6–7 hrs or high 3–4 hrs)
**Yield:** ~7 servings
**Effort:** Dump and forget

Mediterranean take on the classic "crack chicken" — creamy, garlicky shredded chicken, but swaps ranch packets and bacon for sun-dried tomatoes, spinach, and lemon.

---

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed), chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 8 oz light cream cheese, cubed
- 3–4 cups fresh spinach (or 1 cup frozen)
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan (optional, for extra richness)

## Instructions

1. Place chicken thighs in the slow cooker. Add broth, sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, Italian seasoning, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
2. Cook on **low 6–7 hours** or **high 3–4 hours** until chicken shreds easily.
3. Remove chicken, shred with two forks, return to pot.
4. Add cubed cream cheese directly to the hot liquid. Stir on low for 5–10 min until fully melted and creamy.
5. Stir in spinach until wilted (~5 min) and lemon juice. Stir in parmesan if using.
6. Taste and adjust salt.

## Storage

Transfer to 2 large containers. Good for 5–6 days refrigerated, or freeze extra portions — this batch reheats well from frozen.

## Serving

Scoop over lemon herb rice. Already creamy, so no cheese topping needed (skip feta).
