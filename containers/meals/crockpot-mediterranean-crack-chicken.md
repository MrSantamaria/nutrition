# Crockpot Mediterranean "Crack" Chicken

**Method:** Slow cooker (low 6–7 hrs, do not exceed — see note)
**Yield:** ~7 servings
**Effort:** Dump and forget

Mediterranean take on the classic "crack chicken" — creamy, garlicky shredded chicken, with sun-dried tomatoes, spinach, lemon, and real turkey bacon + smoked paprika standing in for the classic bacon flavor.

> **⚠️ Cream cheese must be LIGHT/REDUCED-FAT, not full-fat.** Diego has no gallbladder and full-fat dairy causes GI distress. Instacart has substituted full-fat before — double-check the substitution before checkout or at delivery. See [preferences.md](../../preferences.md).

---

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed), chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 8 oz **light/reduced-fat** cream cheese, cubed (confirm package says "light" or "reduced fat" — not regular)
- 3 slices turkey bacon, cooked crisp and crumbled
- 3–4 cups fresh spinach (or 1 cup frozen) — reserve, see step 5
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan (optional, for extra richness)

## Instructions

1. Place chicken thighs in the slow cooker. Add broth, sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
2. Cook on **low 6 hours** (start checking at 6, don't push to 7+ — overcooking is what makes leftovers mushy). Chicken should shred easily but not fall apart into mush.
3. Remove chicken, shred into large chunks with two forks (don't shred fine), return to pot.
4. Add cubed light cream cheese directly to the hot liquid. Stir on low for 5–10 min until fully melted and creamy.
5. Stir in lemon juice and crumbled turkey bacon. Stir in parmesan if using. **Do not stir in the spinach yet** — see Serving below.
6. Taste and adjust salt.

## Storage

Transfer to 2 large containers **without spinach mixed in**. Good for 5–6 days refrigerated, or freeze extra portions. Keep the reserved spinach separate in a small bag/container in the fridge.

## Serving

Scoop over lemon herb rice. When reheating each portion, stir in a small handful of fresh spinach at the end just until wilted (~1 min) — this keeps it from going slimy/mushy over the week instead of wilting the whole batch up front. Already creamy, so no cheese topping needed (skip feta).
