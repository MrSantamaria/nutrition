# Turkey Sausage & Bacon Breakfast Burritos

**Method:** Stovetop (bacon → sausage + veggies → scramble) + assembly line
**Yield:** 7 burritos
**Effort:** ~40 minutes active

Variation of the [Freezer Breakfast Burritos](breakfast-burritos.md) with turkey
sausage and turkey bacon added. Cheese is cut back slightly and most of the
cooking oil dropped, since the meats bring their own fat.

---

## Ingredients

- 14 large eggs
- 1 lb ground turkey breakfast sausage (roll or bulk, mild)
- 14 slices turkey bacon (2 per burrito)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 bell pepper, diced small
- 1/2 yellow onion, diced small
- 1 cup shredded Mexican-blend cheese
- 7 large (12") flour tortillas (burrito size)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Salsa for serving (not rolled in — gets soggy)

## Instructions

1. **Turkey bacon:** Cook in a large skillet over medium heat, ~3–4 min per side, until crisp at the edges. Work in batches. Set on a paper towel, then chop into bite-size pieces.
2. **Sausage:** In the same skillet, brown the turkey sausage over medium heat, breaking it into small crumbles, ~6–8 min until no pink remains.
3. **Veggies + beans:** Add diced pepper and onion to the sausage, cook until soft, ~5 min. Stir in black beans to warm through, ~2 min. Transfer everything to a bowl.
4. **Eggs:** Whisk all 14 eggs with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Wipe out the skillet, add the olive oil over medium-low, pour in the eggs and stir gently until just set (soft scramble). Pull them slightly early — they'll keep cooking.
5. **Assembly line:** Lay out 7 tortillas. Divide eggs evenly, then the sausage/bean mixture, then the chopped bacon, then cheese.
6. **Roll:** Fold sides in, then roll from the bottom up tightly.
7. **Wrap:** Roll each burrito tightly in aluminum foil.
8. **Freeze:** Place foil-wrapped burritos in a gallon zip-lock bag. Keep 2 in the fridge for the first couple of days, freeze the rest.

## Reheating

- **From freezer:** Remove foil, wrap in damp paper towel, microwave 2.5–3 minutes, flipping halfway (these are fuller than the original — give them the extra 30 sec).
- **From fridge:** Remove foil, wrap in damp paper towel, microwave 1.5 minutes.
- Add salsa after reheating.
