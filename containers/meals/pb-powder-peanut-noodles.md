# PB Powder Peanut Noodles

**Method:** Stovetop
**Yield:** ~7 servings
**Effort:** ~25 minutes active

Noodles tossed in a peanut sauce made from powdered peanut butter (PB2 or
similar) instead of the jar — same peanut flavor, a fraction of the fat and
calories of a traditional oil-heavy peanut sauce.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 lb whole wheat spaghetti or soba noodles
- 3/4 cup PB powder (e.g. PB2)
- 3/4 cup warm water, plus more to thin
- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp fresh grated ginger (or 1 tsp ground)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1–2 tbsp lime juice
- Sliced scallions, for topping (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook noodles per package directions. Drain, rinse briefly under cool water to stop the cooking (helps them hold up through a week of reheats).
2. Whisk PB powder with the warm water until smooth. Whisk in soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, ginger, garlic, sesame oil, and lime juice. Add a splash more water if it's thicker than a pourable sauce.
3. Toss noodles with the sauce until evenly coated.
4. Portion into 2 containers.

## Serving

Top with baked chicken and roasted broccoli. Scallions or a pinch of sesame seeds if you have them.

## Storage

Refrigerate up to 4–5 days. The sauce thickens when cold — loosen with a splash of water when reheating.

## Nutrition (per serving, noodles + sauce only — ~1/7 batch)

| Nutrient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | ~240 |
| Protein | ~12g |
| Carbs | ~43g |
| Fat | ~3g |

## Tips

- PB powder is the swap that keeps this light — regular peanut butter would roughly double the fat and calories in the sauce for the same flavor intensity.
- Rinsing the noodles after draining keeps them from clumping into a starchy block by day 3–4.
