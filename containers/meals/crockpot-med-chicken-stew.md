# Crockpot Mediterranean Chicken Stew

**Method:** Slow cooker (low 6–8 hrs or high 3–4 hrs)
**Yield:** ~7 servings
**Effort:** Dump and forget

---

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 yellow onion, roughly diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1/2 cup kalamata olives, halved
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 3–4 cups fresh spinach (or 1 cup frozen)
- Juice of 1 lemon

## Instructions

1. Place chicken thighs in the bottom of the slow cooker.
2. Add chickpeas, crushed tomatoes, onion, garlic, olives, olive oil, and all spices. Stir gently around the chicken.
3. Cook on **low 6–8 hours** or **high 3–4 hours** until chicken shreds easily.
4. Remove chicken, shred with two forks, return to pot.
5. Stir in spinach and lemon juice. Let wilt for 5 minutes.
6. Taste and adjust salt.

## Storage

Keep in the crockpot insert in the fridge (covered), or transfer to 2–3 large containers. Good for 5–6 days refrigerated.

## Serving

Scoop over lemon herb rice or eat on its own. Top with feta crumbles.
