# Freezer Breakfast Burritos

**Method:** Stovetop scramble + assembly line
**Yield:** 7 burritos
**Effort:** ~30 minutes active

---

## Ingredients

- 14 large eggs
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 bell pepper, diced small
- 1/2 yellow onion, diced small
- 1.5 cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese
- 7 large (12") flour tortillas (burrito size)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Salsa for serving (not rolled in — gets soggy)

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Saute diced pepper and onion until soft, ~5 minutes.
2. Add black beans, stir to warm through, ~2 minutes. Transfer to a bowl.
3. Whisk all 14 eggs with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
4. Heat remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in the same skillet over medium-low. Pour in eggs, stir gently with a spatula until just set (soft scramble). Remove from heat slightly early — they'll keep cooking.
5. **Assembly line:** Lay out 7 tortillas. Divide eggs evenly, then bean/pepper mixture, then cheese.
6. **Roll:** Fold sides in, then roll from bottom up tightly (like a burrito).
7. **Wrap:** Roll each burrito tightly in aluminum foil.
8. **Freeze:** Place foil-wrapped burritos in a gallon zip-lock bag. Freeze.

## Reheating

- **From freezer:** Remove foil, wrap in damp paper towel, microwave 2–2.5 minutes, flipping halfway.
- **From fridge:** Remove foil, wrap in damp paper towel, microwave 1–1.5 minutes.
- Add salsa after reheating.
