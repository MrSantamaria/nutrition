# Quick Chicken Quesadillas

**Category:** Complete meal — casual/snack, made fresh, not batch-prepped
**Method:** Stovetop
**Yield:** 1 quesadilla per person, scale as needed
**Effort:** ~10 minutes

Uses the quesadilla-size tortillas and reduced-fat melting cheese from the
shopping list. Good way to use up leftover baked chicken thighs from the
week's bowls.

---

## Ingredients (per quesadilla)

- 1 quesadilla-size flour tortilla
- 1/3 cup reduced-fat shredded Mexican blend cheese
- 1/2 cup cooked chicken, shredded or chopped (leftover baked chicken thighs work well)
- Optional: diced bell pepper or onion
- 1 tsp olive oil or cooking spray, for the pan

## Instructions

1. Heat a nonstick skillet or griddle over medium heat, lightly coated with oil or cooking spray.
2. Sprinkle cheese over one half of the tortilla, top with chicken and any veggies, fold in half.
3. Cook 2–3 min per side, pressing gently with a spatula, until golden and crispy and the cheese is fully melted.
4. Slice into wedges.

## Serving

Serve with salsa, and a veggie-and-salsa side if you want something lighter alongside.

## Nutrition (per quesadilla, with chicken — estimated)

| Nutrient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Calories | ~420 |
| Protein | ~35g |
| Carbs | ~28g |
| Fat | ~16g |

*Without chicken (cheese-only quesadilla), expect roughly ~280 kcal, 12g protein, 27g carbs, 14g fat.*

## Tips

- Use a reduced-fat cheese labeled as melting well — some low-fat shredded cheeses go rubbery instead of melting smoothly.
- Press gently with the spatula while cooking for even, crispy browning.
