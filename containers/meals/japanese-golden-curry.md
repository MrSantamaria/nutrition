# Japanese Golden Curry (Vegetable Base)

**Method:** Stovetop
**Yield:** ~7 servings
**Effort:** ~35 minutes active

---

## Ingredients

- 1 medium box S&B Golden Curry blocks (7.4 oz) — use the box water ratio printed on back
- 2 medium onions, diced
- 3 large carrots, peeled and chopped into 1-inch chunks
- 2 medium potatoes (Yukon Gold or russet), peeled and cubed
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onions, cook until softened (~5 min).
2. Add carrots and potatoes. Add water per box instructions (typically ~3.5 cups for one box).
3. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cover and cook until potatoes are fork-tender, ~15–20 min.
4. Turn off heat. Break curry blocks into pot and stir until fully dissolved.
5. Return to low heat, simmer uncovered ~5 min until thickened. Portion into 2 large tubs.

## Serving

Reheat curry in microwave (~2–3 min). Air fry 2 frozen panko chicken patties at 400°F for 8–10 min.
Serve rice in bowl, ladle curry over, crispy chicken on top or alongside.

## Storage

Refrigerator: 5 days. Freezer: 3 months.
